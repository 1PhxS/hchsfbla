Houston County High School FBLA provides students with opportunities to develop leadership skills, explore careers, compete, serve their school and community, and connect with other future business leaders.

WHO WE ARE

Our Story & Mission

FBLA brings business and education together through leadership and career development opportunities.

At Houston County High School, our chapter gives students the chance to grow as leaders while becoming involved in competitions, conferences, service projects, and school activities.

We believe that leadership isn't just about holding a position. It's about showing up, helping others, taking initiative, and making an impact.
